# Heritage-Garden

This project is the final outcome of our Software Engineering course, where we explored innovative ways to enhance communication and address daily household challenges within a community, whether it's a small society or a large building with numerous flats.

**Key Features:**

* **Enhanced Community Communication:** Facilitates effective communication among residents through various channels.
* **Efficient Issue Resolution:** Streamlines the process of reporting and resolving common household issues. 

**My Contributions:**

As the team lead, I played a crucial role in driving the project's development. My primary focus was on the backend, where I utilized Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB to build a robust and scalable server-side infrastructure.

**Tech Stack:**

* **Frontend:** React.js 
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** MongoDB
